## Return on investment (1-acre grape farm)

- Revenue: INR 8-12 lakh per acre per year; one damaging rain event costs INR 3-8 lakh; 1-2 events per harvest season.
- Coverage: 4-6 units of 50x50 ft -> INR 4.2-6.3 lakh investment.
- Preventing a single INR 5 lakh loss pays the system back within 1-2 seasons.
- Over 10 years with INR 3,000-5,000 annual service per unit, the protection costs about INR 2,500-4,000 per month.
